STORAGE & FILESYSTEMS
---------------------

COMMAND | PURPOSE                                                      | EXAMPLE

df      | Show free and used space on filesystems                      | df -h
du      | Estimate file and directory space usage                      | du -sh Downloads
lsblk   | List block devices and partitions                            | lsblk
blkid   | Display block-device attributes and filesystem identifiers   | blkid
mount   | Attach a filesystem to a directory                           | mount
umount  | Detach a mounted filesystem                                  | umount /mnt/drive
